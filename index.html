01 / About

Research depth.
Engineering practice.

I build and study machine-learning systems, with a particular interest in how they learn from feedback and how people decide to rely on them.

At Huawei, I work on LLM post-training, model evaluation and retrieval. Previously, I led applied ML work at Sber and developed predictive analytics at VK / MY.GAMES. My earlier work in C++ and computational mechanics gave me a foundation in numerical methods and scientific modelling.

Alongside engineering, I am a PhD student in AI & Machine Learning at ITMO University. My research covers evaluator reliability, game-theoretic models of human oversight and the identifiability of human preferences.
